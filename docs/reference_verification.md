# Reference verification log

Project: "Performance Evaluation of Hybrid CPU-GPU Parallelism for Deep Learning Using OpenMP and CUDA" (CS 259).
Verified: 2026-11-15. Result: 22 entries in `report/overleaf/refs.bib`.

## How entries were verified

Every bibliographic field was taken from a fetched source, not from memory. Author lists and page ranges were also compared programmatically between `refs.bib` and the Crossref record (all 16 DOI-bearing journal/conference entries with a Crossref author list matched in author count, order and family names; pages and volume/issue matched, apart from the notes below).

URL templates used in the table (all were fetched in this session):

- **CR** = `https://api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>` (publisher-deposited metadata)
- **OA** = `https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/<DOI>` (independent second source for authors/volume/issue/pages; also abstracts)
- **S2** = `https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:<DOI>`

The DOI is part of each formatted citation. Sources that could not be fetched (HTTP 403 or bot protection): ACM Digital Library, DBLP, IEEE Xplore, Springer Link, ACM Queue site. Those are not claimed as sources anywhere below.

Section names in the last column are proposed from the experiment list (no draft exists in the repo yet); renumber to match the final paper.

- nickolls2008scalable: only Crossref and OpenAlex (which agree); queue.acm.org blocked.
- hoefler2015scientific: pages 73:1-73:12 come from the author's own page; the ACM page was not fetched.
- yoo2003slurm: LNCS volume 2862 from OpenAlex and a web search result; Springer's page was not fetchable.
- harris2012*: month taken from the date displayed on each page (Dec 04, Nov 07, Dec 13, 2012); the raw HTML timestamps are UTC and fall one day later. These are web posts, not archival; NVIDIA modified them later (HTML `dateModified` 2022-2023) but the displayed publication dates and titles are as cited.
- nvidia2026cudaguide: living document. Cite the version and "last updated" date given; the section numbers above refer to v13.5.
- dagum1998openmp: first names come from the article byline; Crossref and Semantic Scholar carry only initials or variants ("Ram"). The printed article may hyphenate "Industry-Standard"; the publisher record (Crossref/IEEE) has "industry standard", which is what is cited.
- karp1990measuring and williams2009roofline: Karp-Flatt content confirmed from the abstract only; Roofline content from a course-hosted PDF copy, not the publisher page.
- jouppi2017tpu: 76 authors printed in full. Under `cs259.bst` this takes roughly ten lines. If space matters, replace everything after `Patterson, David` in the `author` field with `and others` (prints "et al.").
- kingma2015adam: the DOI field holds the arXiv DOI (identifies the preprint), not a proceedings DOI.
